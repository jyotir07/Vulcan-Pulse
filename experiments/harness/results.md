Synthetic data; simulation output, not real payment performance. Generated by `python scripts/evaluate.py`.

- Train days: 2026-01-05 to 2026-01-27
- Test days: 2026-01-28 to 2026-02-03
- Counterfactual windows: 2026-01-28, 2026-02-03
- Scenarios: 42 per window (49 in range, 35 out of range, over both windows)
- Out of range: more than 5% of affected transactions have a state feature outside the training 0.1%–99.9% range
